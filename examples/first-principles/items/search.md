Order gives you information about places you have not examined.

# Divide the possibilities

Open a dictionary near its middle. If your word comes earlier, the later half cannot contain it. Repeat with the remaining half.

- Each comparison removes many candidate positions.
- The method needs a consistent sorting rule.
- An empty remaining range means the word is absent.

A useful representation makes useful procedures possible.

A sorted collection trades preparation for faster lookup.
